How to set up Supabase (Free, 2 mins)

1. Go to supabase.com and sign in / create a free project.

2. In your dashboard, click Storage in the left sidebar.

3. Click "New Bucket", name it birthday-photos, and turn on the "Public bucket" switch!

4. Go to Project Settings (gear icon) ➔ API, copy your Project URL and anon public key.

5. Paste them above and click Save Settings. All photos you upload will automatically host on Supabase so Loukya sees them on her device!
